Show me where we are, by outcome, through the where_card tool. No edits, no next step started, no plan; the tool lays the card out.

- The ladder is the rungs `target`, `higher`, `beyond`, `slice`, `this`, in that order. `target` is the outcome I want, in my latest words. `higher` is what the effort lands in or serves (a branch to merge, a release, a programme). `beyond` is the effort the slice belongs to. `slice` is the agreed batch in hand. `this` is the one piece in hand right now; when that piece is finished, `this` is the next open piece.
- Leave out a rung we never named; `target` and two rungs is a fine answer. Never invent a rung, a check, a count or a percentage.
- A rung's outcome states what is true when it is done, not the activity. Its checks are its done-when checks, each something I could verify, taken from what we agreed. If we never agreed how a rung is done, its only check is the open check "done-when not agreed", closed by a `needs you` action.
- A check appears once, on the lowest rung that owns it. A higher rung lists the rung below it as one check, then only what remains beyond it. At most 5 checks on a rung.
- Check states: `done` only with proof from this session: a sha or path from the facts below, test output you saw, or a memory id. Proof I cannot find is shown as unverified. `reported`: done according to a todo, a summary or your own earlier "done", but not checked; say where. `doing` and `open` name the actions that close them.
- Actions are numbered, verb first, one move each. Every open check has at least one action, and every action closes a check. At most 9.
- `needs you`: my moves (decisions, approvals, things to try), with the options and your pick, or "none yet". `I do next`: what you will do once I answer, with what it needs first ("needs nothing", "after 1-2", "your commit"). `waiting`: what cannot start yet, with until what.
- `ideas`: your suggestions I have not agreed to, one line; never a check or an action. `reply`: one example of how I could answer by number, and what you would do then.
- A brief (when I name an action or a rung) has `what`, `where`, `why`, `how`, `effort`, `impact`, `pick`, `context`. `effort` and `impact` come from what we measured or said; write "not sized" rather than guess.
- My latest words win over the original ask and over a compaction summary. Where you know a summary lost a rung or a check we had named, write "lost in compaction" in it. Leave out a rung you only suspect.
- Keep each text short: the tool cuts anything that does not fit 100 columns.
